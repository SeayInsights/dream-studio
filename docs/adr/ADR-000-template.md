## Context

What is the problem, force, or constraint driving this decision? State the facts and
tensions — not the solution. Enough that a future reader understands *why* a decision
was needed without external context.

## Decision

The decision, in the active voice: "We will …". State exactly what is chosen. If
alternatives were seriously considered, name them and say briefly why they lost.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include follow-on work,
new obligations, and risks accepted. Honest trade-offs, not just benefits.
